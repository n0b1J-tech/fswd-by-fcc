Butterchicken Recipie

how to butter chicken?

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Butter
  • Heavy Cream
  • Ginger,Garlic paste
  • Tomato puree
  • Garam Masala

Instructions

  1. Sear the chicken: Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear until browned on all sides (about 5 minutes). Remove the chicken from the pan.
  2. Build the base: Reduce heat to medium. Melt the remaining butter, add the ginger-garlic paste and garam masala, and fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Simmer: Pour in the tomato puree and a pinch of salt. Bring to a simmer, then return the chicken to the pan. Cook for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce thickens.
  4. Finish: Stir in the heavy cream and remove from heat.
